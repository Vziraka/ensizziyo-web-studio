# Visual research and art direction

Read before new website or campaign directions. The owner wants creative depth, expressive typography and compositions that feel designed for the company. Treat a tidy template as an unfinished starting point. A small local business deserves the same visual care as a larger brand; clarity and originality belong together.

## Inspect references before drawing

Start with the owner's supplied or previously selected references, then inspect these sources for the current brief:

- [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/): discover complete sites; follow suitable entries to inspect the real opening and what follows it.
- [Godly](https://godly.website/) / [Recent websites](https://recent.design/websites): explore alternative visual structures. Follow the live destination rather than relying only on a gallery thumbnail. Honor the current redirect rather than assuming the old catalog is unchanged.
- [21st.dev](https://21st.dev/): inspect relevant component or interaction previews. Use them to explore a detail or behavior within an original page, not to assemble the same library landing page for every company.

Build a compact set of useful references across sources, usually 4-6. Seek both business relevance and unexpected but transferable craft from adjacent fields. A service-company brief should not be limited to other generic service-company sites. Study composition, typography, image direction, section transitions and how the customer action stays clear. Avoid choosing every reference for the same oversized sans-serif hero or fashionable effect.

Record each selected reference's title/URL, access date, screenshot or viewed state where available, a specific observed design principle, and its intended application to a direction. A list of gallery homepages, search snippets or remembered styles is not a completed visual research pass. Include a concise reference-to-direction note in the review; detailed captures stay in the private task record. Reference brands, artwork and customer claims are never company assets.

If a catalog, destination or preview is blocked, use another accessible source or an already inspected capture and state its age/limits. Do not imply that blocked content was viewed. No new account, purchase, component installation or hosting is needed for this research. Retain useful references for subsequent refinements instead of repeating research on every small edit.

## Give each direction a visual premise

Before full composition, make a compact comparison of the directions: customer emphasis, defining visual idea, layout silhouette, headline/body font families, image treatment, supporting-section rhythm, and reference principles. This is an internal working brief, not another approval gate. The entries must describe visible decisions, not five mood adjectives.

Choose actual type families and treatments for each direction. Explore differences in letterform, width, weight, case, scale, spacing, line breaks and editorial voice. A shared body family can be practical, but five concepts with the same headline family and hierarchy do not provide the requested typographic exploration unless the user explicitly fixes that system. Do not default to Manrope, Inter or a system sans because it is already loaded, and do not replace them with a different universal default. Use available licensed fonts or an appropriate openly licensed source, confirm the intended faces actually render, and retain license information. Keep families purposeful within each direction; variety does not mean indiscriminately mixing fonts.

Let the premise determine the composition. Explore changes in image scale and placement, reading path, navigation framing, relationships between type and photography, density and negative space, and the way sections connect. Structure should still communicate the offer and action. Preserve any user-requested mix of grounded and adventurous directions without making the grounded set generic.

The adventurous end of a comparison should change the relationship between imagery, typography and space across the page. An irregular gallery, photographic montage, spatial composition or unusual editorial index can be useful exploration prompts when appropriate; they are not assigned styles. A sidebar or extra-bold heading alone does not make the set adventurous. Include the most expressive study in the opening comparison instead of polishing only familiar options.

Build visual depth through a coherent relationship between foreground and background, contrasting scales, framing, purposeful overlap, considered crops, material or graphic details drawn from the business, and changes of pace across the page. Use the techniques the premise needs; do not add all of them. Depth can be editorial or spatial. Random shadows, blobs, gradients, ornament, 3D objects or animation do not repair a weak composition.

Keep original company imagery directly placed and proportionally scaled. Reconsider crop, sequence, context and supporting details before requesting different assets. Do not solve a difficult composition by inventing the company's people, projects or products. Expressive layout and typography are compatible with exact photographs and an unchanged logo.

## Design the page before packaging it

Develop opening studies at realistic desktop proportions and compare them at the same scale. Continue independently once they work; do not stop to request approval for this internal exploration. Complete the requested supporting sections using the same visual language, with deliberate changes of pace instead of repeating equal boxes.

Use authoring tools that support the desired typography, layout and imagery. Local HTML/CSS with browser capture is often a useful route for rich page studies; it remains static mockup authoring with no hosting requirement. Native PDF/vector composition is suitable when it can match the visual intent. Share low-level utilities for asset placement and export, not a single hero, grid, font setup and footer template that flattens every direction. Keep PDF captions and review notes outside the proposed customer-facing page.

If motion matters, design a meaningful still state and describe the proposed change, trigger and customer purpose. A short sequence of stills can explain the idea when useful. An animation note cannot supply visual interest that is absent from the rendered design.

## Rework before delivery

Review the actual renders against the selected references and the company's brief, separately from export correctness:

- **Composition:** compare small opening thumbnails and the full pages. If the same silhouette and reading path repeat, redesign the nearest pair. A new color, circular photo or decorative flow line on the same split hero is not a new layout.
- **Typography:** inspect real glyphs and headline settings. Distinct font names in notes are insufficient if the rendered hierarchy and character still look the same. Check intended fonts loaded and line breaks were designed.
- **Identity and depth:** name the concrete visual idea and how it develops through the page. If replacing the logo and photos would leave an interchangeable template, develop the business-specific composition further.
- **Continuity:** check the supporting sections and campaign materials carry the premise. One dramatic opening above generic service cards is unfinished.
- **Usability and craft:** verify readable text, clear actions, purposeful contrast and spacing, strong photo crops and restrained detail at the intended viewing scale. Complexity is not a substitute for care.

Rework weak or repetitive studies before including them. Do not fill a quota with a deliberately safe option. Record concrete shortcomings and revisions rather than assigning an unsupported aesthetic score. If a material source or tool limitation remains, describe the resulting compromise honestly. A successful image count, source hash or PDF bounds check does not establish this visual-design pass.
