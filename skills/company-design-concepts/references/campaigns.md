# Campaign directions

Use for promotional concepts for the **client company**. This mode is not Ensizziyo prospecting or outreach execution.

## Establish the promotion

Use the shared company research to identify the audience, product/service/event or offer, desired customer action, useful evidence, and destination. Infer a sensible goal when sources support it and state the assumption. Ask a focused question if the missing goal or offer would materially change the campaign.

A campaign idea may propose a new message or emphasis. A new discount, event date, price, deadline, or commercial commitment requires verified information or clear proposal labeling. Never present an imagined promotion as an existing company offer.

## Three coherent ideas

Compare **three campaign ideas** for the same agreed promotion/goal. Each should have a different audience insight, creative hook, story, or persuasion approach, grounded in the actual business. Three color variations of one ad do not constitute three ideas.

For each idea, create the following **sample designs**:

1. **Social post:** a designed post using appropriate company imagery, a short headline where useful, and an example caption.
2. **Ad:** a visual ad sample with a clear message and customer action. It can share campaign imagery with the post while showing how the paid treatment works.
3. **Landing-page concept:** the opening and enough supporting content to demonstrate message continuity, relevant evidence, and the next customer action. It is a static concept, not a built campaign destination.

Keep imagery, offer wording, tone, and visual identity coherent across all three materials within each idea. Use the actual company logo and image files in the final studies. Separate the three ideas clearly so the review compares complete promotional approaches instead of nine unrelated visuals.

Choose sensible sample formats for the intended channels. Treat sizes and placements as concept choices; do not claim current platform compliance or finished export readiness without the separate checks those claims require.

## Explain and recommend

Give each idea a stable name and a short account of the intended audience, message, supporting evidence, action, and why it suits the business. Note an important tradeoff or missing asset where it affects the choice. Avoid invented reach, revenue, conversion, or return-on-ad-spend predictions.

Prospect presentations emphasize the visual idea and business relevance. Paying-client presentations add the reasoning and how the post, ad, and landing-page concept work together. Recommend one approach against the agreed goal.

The requested deliverable is a **PDF of ideas and sample designs only**. Render actual samples into the PDF; prose descriptions and lists of future assets are insufficient. Do not automatically create an upload-ready asset package, media-buying plan, posting schedule, email sequence, or live campaign. Later requests can select, refine, or separately authorize additional work.
