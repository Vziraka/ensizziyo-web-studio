# Pricing and commercial defaults

Owner interview and master approval: October 20, 2026. These are internal starting points for affordable small-business work, not automatic client quotes or proof that a capability has been delivered. The owner explicitly accepted a $1,500 standard website with a $1,000 starter. The broader recommended catalog below is retained for project discussion under the same approval requirement.

## Starting catalog (USD)

| --- | --- | --- |

Complex apps, custom software, larger integrations, data migrations, ongoing social management, SEO and advertising management require their own estimates. The skill covers those services; lack of a catalog number is not a reason to force them into a website or content package.

The approved master illustrates an additional page at $250 once and a booking embed/connection at $350 once. Treat these as starting suggestions, not universal prices: an embedded calendar and a custom two-way booking integration are different work.

Earlier pipeline offers may show $1,500-$2,500 systems work or $199/month care including 60 minutes. Those are different scopes. Do not silently replace an existing offer or contract with the smaller new entry package, and do not change dashboard configuration or public prices through this skill.

## Build the project estimate

- Start with scope, not the client's apparent ability to pay. Use one recommendation and clearly separated optional add-ons.
- Assume one business location and client-supplied content only when confirmed or explicitly discussed. Specify pages, flows, asset counts, tools, integrations and testing.
- Estimate discovery, research/design, implementation, testing, revisions, project communication, handover and defect-fix effort. Check the effective return after direct costs and realistic rework. The owner has not set a universal hourly rate.
- Show material adjustments and their reasons in the internal review. Avoid charging twice for overlapping scope when bundling.
- For a limited budget, propose a smaller useful scope or phased delivery. Preserve thoughtful design and reliable testing; do not make the low-cost package deliberately generic.
- Prices in this guide are starting points. Do not invent discounts, claim a package is "most popular", or imply all projects fit these ranges. Resolve material unknowns before asking for final approval; use an explicit approved allowance only when appropriate.

## Agreed commercial rules

- **50% upfront and 50% before launch.** For non-launch work, discuss and use the equivalent final-delivery/handover milestone. Calculate both amounts in cents; if an odd cent remains, assign it to the final installment so the total reconciles.
- **Two consolidated revision rounds per project**, with their allocation defined in scope. They are not two rounds for every asset/page/feature. New features, additional pages and replacement directions are separately scoped; quote changes before work begins.
- **30 calendar days of defect fixes after launch/delivery**, for reproducible failures against the delivered agreed scope. Defects do not consume revision rounds. New requirements, client changes and third-party outages/platform changes are separately assessed.
- **One handover session** plus useful documentation. The master demonstrates up to 60 minutes; confirm the duration and attendees for each proposal.
- **Care is optional.** State whether any ongoing monitoring, editing or support is purchased. Do not automatically add a retainer.
- **Clients pay third-party costs separately**, preferably in their own accounts: domain, hosting, CRM, automation platform, AI/telephony usage, messaging, paid assets and advertising spend as applicable.
- Verify current vendor plans and expected recurring/usage costs when proposing actual tools. Separate verified prices, estimated consumption and unknowns. Show who pays, the billing frequency and any approved limits. The fictional master contains no verified vendor budget.
- State whether any applicable taxes/fees are included or additional when relevant; do not invent tax treatment. Do not invent non-refundable deposits, cancellation penalties, guarantees, recurring commitments or other material terms.

## Dated research context (not a live market rate card)

Research reviewed October 20, 2026. Advertised prices indicate what sellers ask; historical contract ranges and client-reported costs are different evidence. Worldwide marketplace rates are not a DMV buyer median. Refresh relevant sources if asked for current market research, if stale evidence would materially affect a quote, or when a vendor/cost assumption changes. Do not rerun broad research for every routine proposal.

- [Week One Sites](https://weekonesites.com/pricing): advertised $1,000 for up to five pages with one revision.

- [Kodiak Graphics, Northern Virginia](https://www.kodiakgraphicsva.com/pricing/): advertised $1,200+ one page and $3,000-$5,000+ for 3-10 pages.
- [Icebreaker CRM setup](https://icebreaker.agency/productized-services/crm-lead-capture-setup/): advertised $750 form routing, $1,200 pipeline foundation, $1,600 with booking.

- [Upwork automation](https://www.upwork.com/hire/marketing-automation-freelancers/cost/) and [chatbot rates](https://www.upwork.com/hire/chatbot-developers/cost/): published historical worldwide contract bands of $40-$90/hour and $30-$61/hour respectively, not fixed-price project medians.
- [PointWake](https://pointwake.com/services/ai-voice-agents): advertised $997 setup plus $297/month including 500 minutes; [Endorithm](https://www.endorithm.com/pricing): $1,500 setup plus $299/month. Bundled operation differs from EVZ's client-paid software model.

- [San Juan Agency](https://thesanjuanagency.com/products/social-media-management-subscription): advertised management from $499/month with a three-month term; not equivalent to six bespoke content assets.
- [Paid Pilot](https://paidpilot.net/pricing/): advertised limited ads management from $499/month, ad spend separate.
- [Ahrefs provider survey](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-pricing/): a 2024 survey reported approximately $1,557/month for local SEO on average. Older seller-survey data, not a current small-business transaction benchmark.
- [Clutch web design costs](https://clutch.co/web-designers/pricing): verified client-review evidence with widely varying scopes; its aggregate cannot establish a five-page local-business price.

Keep market research and internal margin calculations out of the client proposal unless the owner requests a relevant explanation.
