# Website implementation brief

## Audience and offer

Primary: owner-operated and small local service businesses in Fairfax and the DMV. Secondary: small agencies needing a development partner. The first outreach pilot should focus on one service-business category, selected from observed demand and reachable owners.

Offer hypothesis: polished custom websites and landing pages that make services clear and make requesting an estimate or booking easier. Starting project-price hypothesis: $1,500–$2,000+, with a written scope before quoting. No guaranteed lead or revenue claims.

Optional, separately scoped services: ongoing website care, campaign landing pages, advertising creative, campaign management, and business-system integrations. Advertising spend, subscriptions, and ongoing maintenance are distinct from the build price. Custom systems require a defined workflow, data requirements, acceptance criteria, support expectations, and separate estimate.

## First site

Build a focused studio site with offer, services, a clearly labelled concept demonstration, process, founder introduction, FAQ, and one primary inquiry action. Agency partnerships can have a secondary section/contact route. Start with the minimum page count needed to make this understandable.

The demonstration is not a real client engagement. Do not invent testimonials, client logos, years of experience, performance figures, or completed projects.

## Design

Quality references for our own site:
- https://brightmatterdesign.com
- https://bitnomial.com/
- https://www.clarvos.com/

Use deliberate typography, strong spacing, professional imagery, and restrained motion. References establish a quality standard; do not copy their assets, brand, or distinctive page wholesale. Each client gets an audience-appropriate design. Mobile usability, readability, accessibility, and reduced-motion behavior take priority over decorative animation.

Compare two focused directions before building the full site. Browser prototypes are sufficient; a canvas tool is optional. The proposed free Paper alternative is awaiting a link and assessment.

## Technical decisions

Choose the smallest stack that supports the agreed editing and inquiry workflow. Do not add database, authentication, payments, CMS, or custom CRM by default. Prefer existing services for booking and other routine integrations when appropriate. Identify commercial-use hosting terms and ongoing costs before deployment. Do not assume subscription plans include API usage.

## Acceptance criteria

- A visitor can identify the service, who it helps, and the next step.
- Inquiry submission reaches the agreed destination and gives an accurate confirmation; errors are understandable.
- Mobile and desktop layouts have no horizontal overflow or clipped important content.
- Navigation and form controls work by keyboard, with visible focus and labels.
- Motion respects reduced-motion preferences; media is appropriately sized.
- No fabricated proof, broken links, exposed credentials, or test data in production.
- Relevant automated checks and an end-to-end inquiry check pass; security review matches the actual functionality.
- Hosting ownership, costs, editing process, and rollback instructions are documented.
