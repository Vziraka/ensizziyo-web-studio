# Business selection and discovery

Approved October 19 after the owner's interview. This supersedes the earlier food-only research focus; previous evidence and attempts retain their original meaning.

Today offers Home Services; Health & Wellness; Food & Beverage; Real Estate & Property; Professional Services; Other Local Businesses. Optional subtypes narrow the selected group. Existing records are classified from their saved category without rewriting evidence. Unknown categories remain labelled unclassified. Due callbacks and promised next steps remain visible across filters, appear once and count toward the existing 50 unique-business/day total.

Filtering performs no research or provider action. Find more businesses opens a separate scope picker, initially matching the current filter. One request covers 25 candidate businesses total across selected groups, not 25 per group/subtype. Only one active batch runs. Starting a batch is the owner action for its exact selected scope. When calls-only operation is already active, a completed batch can add only its source-reviewed, unrestricted verification cards to that call-task scope, with retained packet/review hashes and approval provenance. A paused call release stays paused. This prepares tasks; it never dials, emails or books a meeting. Ordinary imports retain their separate release behavior. It retains dated search/page receipts, progress, deduplication and explicit held reasons. Current resource limits, website/client priority and the 30% subscription reserve apply. Automatic stocking is deferred.

Research prioritizes the current owner/operator and published professional contact information. General business phones remain labelled general; a sourced owner name does not prove a direct number. Verified identity, offering, DMV locality and a public business phone can support a clearly labelled quick-verification card. Activity, goals, willingness to buy and budget remain unknown until supported. Full pitch-ready qualification retains its separate evidence/review requirements. A 25-candidate batch can produce fewer than 25 usable cards; it does not search indefinitely to fill a quota. Established-business evidence is a preference, not proof of financial capacity; strong small, solo, home-based and pop-up businesses remain eligible.
