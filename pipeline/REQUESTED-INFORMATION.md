## Outcome and bounded scope

An assigned operator records an actual connected call and the business's request, confirms the exact business recipient, prepares a sourced response with optional existing approved PDFs, optionally asks for an AI proposal, reviews the complete draft, and separately approves one exact send. Both operators see the saved request, evidence, draft, attachments and outcome. The request does not create an incoming email, research qualification, new-business outreach count or permission to restart cold cadence.

The first complete increment adds a `requested-information` source and delivery kind only to an explicitly opted-in `shared-operations-desk-v1` policy. Existing policies and every older capability retain their denials. Missing opt-in means no new task, draft approval or send authority; historical records remain readable. Source merge, environment flags, a call outcome and draft adoption cannot opt in implicitly. New PDF generation and audio/transcription remain outside this increment.

Requested information can be commercial. A request to send marketing, examples, pricing or a proposal does not prove a transactional purpose. This increment retains the commercial mailing-address and existing sender/footer requirements for the new delivery kind. It provides no transactional checkbox or automatic exception. Any later transactional exception needs a separately reviewed content/purpose contract and supporting evidence; do not infer one from the request or implement it here. The unresolved address holds real dispatch while allowing request confirmation, drafting, original-PDF inspection and source integration to proceed.

## Caller contract

All mutation inputs are closed schemas with an exact canonical revision. Signed browser actors are `owner` or `friend`, derived from the session. The trusted worker receives only its task authority. Route family: `/api/requested-information/{confirm|draft|approve}`; reuse `/api/mailbox-work/{create|cancel|adopt}` and `/api/mailbox-delivery/{package|approve|dispatch|reconcile}` with explicit source/kind discrimination, rather than exposing the legacy information-send transport. Final field names may follow existing repository conventions without weakening these bindings.

1. **Confirm the request.** Input identifies `project_id`, `call_id`, exact `call_sha256`, confirmed business identity, the exact requested recipient, bounded request text, and explicit identity/recipient/request confirmation attestations. The server derives a source fingerprint and records the actor, event ID and timestamp. The call must be an existing, non-simulated, non-undone, manually attested connected call with a recorded request for information. The confirming actor must currently own the record. A saved `requested_info` outcome is evidence of an operator report, not independent provider proof or blanket approval of every sentence in private notes.
2. **Verification-only cards.** Permit a narrow review of the reported business identity, recipient and actual request from that connected call. Keep their original verification-only qualification and all restrictions. Do not fabricate detailed research, an incoming message or an independently verified call. A newly confirmed email route must be recorded with its actual operator/call provenance; it must not overwrite a historical contact silently. If it differs from the current route, confirm the change explicitly and invalidate dependent approvals. Ambiguous identity, uncertain authority to request information, missing recipient or a conflicting contact remains held.
3. **Save a draft.** Input binds the current request fingerprint and exact recipient, subject, body and optional approved asset/version/digest references. Store an unapproved draft and preserve revisions. Private call notes are context for review, never automatically outgoing text. The draft must address the recorded request without asserting unsupported conversations, goals or results. Changing the confirmed request, identity/contact binding, owner, draft or original attachment baseline requires a new review and approval.
4. **Optional AI proposal.** Create an on-demand task with a discriminated source, such as `{kind:'requested-information', request_id, request_sha256}`. Its context contains the confirmed request, attributed call facts, relevant retained client context, operator writing style and exact eligible PDF catalogue. It does not contain a fabricated `reply`, Message-ID or thread. Preserve existing reply-task validation unchanged. Worker output is a proposal; adoption saves Draft and never approves content or sending. Original PDF reconstruction, whole-file digest and inspection requirements match the existing mailbox worker. Worker credentials cannot confirm requests, change contacts, adopt, approve or send.
5. **Approve content.** Assigned operator explicitly checks the exact recipient, wording, facts and original selected PDF bytes. Bind approval to the current source and draft fingerprints and attachment versions. Ownership changes supersede previous approvals; the new owner must approve again. The nonassigned operator retains shared read-only visibility.
6. **Approve and dispatch the exact package.** Package kind is `requested-information`, with source references to the confirmed request, call/event, current contact/owner, draft and content-approval event. Use no `In-Reply-To`, references or thread ID unless a separately validated real conversation binding exists; the first increment sends a new message. Exact send approval remains distinct from content approval. Require current policy opt-in, sender/grant, source, ownership, restrictions, mailbox coverage, allowance, commercial prerequisites and deadline immediately before POST. A newer relevant inquiry/reply or unresolved identity/source interrupts dispatch for review.

The package must not enter the cold-outreach batch scheduler or acquire three-business-day/seven-calendar-day follow-ups. No automatic campaign enrollment or resurrection follows from confirmation, adoption or sending. Count a verified requested-information send in total outbound and a separate requested-information category; it is neither a new-business first email nor an incoming-thread reply. Reserve and uncertain liabilities still count against shared sender allowances. Existing cold sequence stops remain retained.

## Persistence, failures and recovery

Use typed conditional transitions, complete independent replay and current-source checks at the canonical store boundary. Do not create a second outgoing queue or use `sendInformation` as a shortcut. Reuse immutable MIME storage, permanent reserve/claim-before-POST, exact provider acknowledgement and original Sent readback. Concurrent approval/dispatch, committed lost acknowledgements and request restarts must not duplicate effects. A timeout is Uncertain, never success; reconciliation reads the original deterministic message identity and cannot resend it. Preserve the original sender/account/grant identity for eligible recovery readback.

Request/contact review, content approval and send approval are distinct provenance. Keep prior drafts, source records, claims, charges and receipts through reassignment, pause and recovery. Source hashes must cover the full reviewed evidence, not mutable summary labels. Apply invocation deadlines across credential/source/PDF reads and before CAS/POST. Pause or ownership/source changes during those reads prevent dispatch. Missing/stale originals, revoked grants, capacity limits and failed readbacks produce explicit holds without dropping the underlying call or request.

Portable backup must carry request/draft/approval history and every exact original/MIME dependency. Restore preserves evidence and charges with execution held; it does not recreate approval or a new send intent. Notification projection remains post-validation and capacity-safe. External notifications remain independently held.
