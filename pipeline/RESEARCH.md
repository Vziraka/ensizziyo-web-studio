## Discovery screen before detailed research

SOSA performs a short public-evidence screen before preparing a detailed job. Use the search/capture bounds of the separately authorized run; inspect enough context to make the decision, without conducting a full audit or repeatedly searching to force an idea. If access or key facts cannot be resolved within those bounds, record the exact next verification step and move on.

Check:

1. Identity and fit: verify the business, food/beverage offering and service area. Reuse existing identifiers where available; check existing records for duplicates and do-not-contact restrictions before proposing another job.
2. Activity: retain credible dated first-party evidence of current operation under the existing freshness rules. An undated directory, follower count or review count alone does not establish current activity, revenue or customer demand.
3. Current route and contact: inspect how customers find the menu/products, order, arrange pickup or make a custom-order/catering enquiry, as relevant, including linked existing alternatives. Capture a first-party public business contact route; prioritize phone/email for the planned channels. A public social route can support research while email discovery continues. Do not claim a published phone/email reaches the owner directly or is deliverable without verification.

Call-queue handoff: retain the exact publicly advertised business phone, its first-party source and inspection date; retain email when published, with an empty review value when unavailable. A verified business phone suffices for calls in either lane. Owner/operator-direct professional access is preferred; a general business line remains eligible and must keep its honest label. Email is required for email actions and the separately confirmed booking guest, not for the call card. Do not infer numbers or use private personal data. Existing calls/confirmed bookings retain their owner release; commercial email and the currently paused general-research run keep their separate controls.

Discovery can start from Maps, public business social profiles, market/vendor rosters or professional listings as well as websites. Preserve the discovered-from source separately from verified website status and the observed customer channel; unknown main channel or an absent website button does not establish no website. Retain access failures and the specific next check. For verification calls, a retained named-business directory/market profile may establish DMV locality alongside business-controlled identity, offering and exact public phone proof. It cannot establish those primary claims or an owner-direct number by itself. Source-only review never expands the live call approval; stage a new candidate for separate release review.
4. Specific opening: state one addition/improvement tied to that observed offering or customer step, its possible benefit, the existing alternative and one owner question. Check obvious duplication. A visible failure or owner complaint is optional; unknown goals, budget or buying interest alone do not block advancement.

Keep a compact dated screening note with the policy revision, actual discovery query/URLs, identity/category/area, activity evidence/date, inspected website/profile and customer route, captured contact source, initial idea/basis/alternative/hypothetical benefit/question, and decision/reason/next check. Keep real notes in the private discovery/capture records. When a later run is authorized, carry the summary through the existing supported packet fields; do not add unsupported job/report fields or Sheet columns.

## What makes an opportunity

Tie one practical addition or improvement to an observed offering/customer journey. Consider enhancements even when the existing route works. Explain what the addition could enable and how it complements existing alternatives; check for duplication. For food/beverages, inspect existing menu/storefront, preorder, pickup, messaging and catering tools before proposing another page or system. A small digital presence is a useful discovery signal, not a complete proposal. A generic website/CRM/AI package is insufficient.

Put current facts in observations with citations. In version-2/3 reports, each opportunity supplies observed_basis, a specific proposed_solution, possible_effect as a hypothesis, existing_alternatives, validation_needed and one focused owner_question. Clearly describe whether the proposal is an enhancement or addresses an observed problem. Follow the supplied version's schema exactly; older schemas express checks in their existing owner_question/unknowns fields, without adding fields.

With adequate primary evidence and a supported enhancement, the report can use Needs review while current activity, identity or a usable contact remains unverified. Preserve the idea and exact verification gap. Hold/Pass belong to the independent decision schema; they are not report-recommendation values. Repair a specific unsupported claim without discarding valid uncertainty or raising readiness on unchanged evidence. An Accepted fidelity review can still have a Hold business decision; current dated activity and full contact checks remain necessary for qualification.

Evaluate a tailored enhancement before choosing No strong public case. If none is defensible after adequate inspection, explain why in unknowns. Unknown goals, budget or buying interest alone are not reasons to dismiss a relevant enhancement. Directory-only/unsupported evidence still cannot qualify an opportunity under the evidence contract.

## Decisions and priority

Research-qualified: a specific supported enhancement/problem opportunity, reviewed current primary operating evidence and a captured first-party public contact route, with every observation/journey finding reviewed. This means worth a discovery conversation, not confirmed purchase or a struggling business. Preserve existing freshness, citation, duplicate and qualification checks. Email is primary; a public business social contact can support research while email discovery continues.

Hold: key identity, activity, contact or required supporting evidence is uncertain. Record the exact next verification step. Unknown budget, interest or private workflows alone do not force Hold when the relevant public context and enhancement are supported.

Pass: a verified campaign mismatch/closed business, or adequate research yields no specific defensible enhancement beyond existing alternatives. Record why. Pass applies to this campaign and can be revisited if information changes; insufficient access/evidence belongs on Hold.

Within the food/beverage campaign, first seek the basic/social-first setups described above, then prioritize the clearest tailored idea, credible current activity and a usable contact route. An established food/beverage business with a stronger specific opening can outrank a basic setup with a weak idea. Owner-expressed goals or a relevant new offering can strengthen the case when actually observed; neither is required. Do not invent revenue, affordability, urgency or a numerical score. Evaluate campaign fit through usable conversations, owner-confirmed needs and suitable projects relative to research effort; the number of reports alone does not establish success.
