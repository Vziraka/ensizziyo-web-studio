## Purpose and scope

Find active businesses we could enhance with websites/landing pages, business systems/integrations, practical automation/AI or marketing support. Working businesses, home-based operators and social-first sellers are eligible. A visible failure, complaint, expressed buying interest or confirmed budget is not required to identify an opportunity.

Use: **observed business context → tailored enhancement → plausible benefit → conversation to confirm fit**. An observed problem can also support an opportunity. Both need evidence for the current context; benefits and owner demand remain hypotheses. Do not invent poor performance, lost sales, missing systems or a missing website to justify an enhancement.

The completed ten-business comparison covered four residential cleaners, three food businesses and three independent trades around Fairfax/the DMV. All ten had websites, so that comparison did not establish the fit of social-first/no-verified-site businesses. On October 16 the owner selected residential cleaners for the first outreach campaign, with the discovery screen below. This is a campaign hypothesis, not proof of demand or a permanent restriction on the studio's services. No discovery run, queue release, worker call, schedule or sending starts just because this playbook exists.

## First campaign: residential cleaners

Actively seek owner-operated residential cleaning businesses serving Fairfax/the DMV, including solo and home-based operators. Give first attention to active businesses with a basic digital setup, a mainly Facebook/Instagram presence or no verified standalone website, when there is a specific useful enhancement to explore. Verify residential service and area; do not guess ownership or direct access to the owner from size or appearance. A missing public owner name alone does not exclude a reachable business.

Business success and digital maturity are separate. A successful or established cleaner can be a strong candidate when a particular opportunity is more compelling. A polished website alone is not a reason to exclude it. Limited technology alone does not qualify a business, establish poor performance or imply budget, demand or willingness to buy. Prefer a meaningful addition over a cosmetic tweak or a generic package.

All four studio services remain available. Food vendors/home bakeries and independent trades remain possible later campaigns; preserve existing useful records. Do not mix new discovery from other categories into this first campaign or automatically regrade an older prospect because the focus changed. Record an otherwise relevant non-cleaner for a later campaign.

## Discovery screen before detailed research

SOSA performs a short public-evidence screen before preparing a detailed job. Use the search/capture bounds of the separately authorized run; inspect enough context to make the decision, without conducting a full audit or repeatedly searching to force an idea. If access or key facts cannot be resolved within those bounds, record the exact next verification step and move on.

Check:

1. Identity and fit: verify the business, residential-cleaning offering and service area. Reuse existing identifiers where available; check existing records for duplicates and do-not-contact restrictions before proposing another job.
2. Activity: retain credible dated first-party evidence of current operation under the existing freshness rules. An undated directory, follower count or review count alone does not establish current activity, revenue or customer demand.
3. Current route and contact: inspect how customers learn about services and request an estimate/booking, including linked existing alternatives. Capture a first-party public business contact route; prioritize phone/email for the planned channels. A public social route can support research while email discovery continues. Do not claim a published phone/email reaches the owner directly or is deliverable without verification.
4. Specific opening: state one addition/improvement tied to that observed offering or customer step, its possible benefit, the existing alternative and one owner question. Check obvious duplication. A visible failure or owner complaint is optional; unknown goals, budget or buying interest alone do not block advancement.

Keep a compact dated screening note with the policy revision, actual discovery query/URLs, identity/category/area, activity evidence/date, inspected website/profile and customer route, captured contact source, initial idea/basis/alternative/hypothetical benefit/question, and decision/reason/next check. Keep real notes in the private discovery/capture records. When a later run is authorized, carry the summary through the existing supported packet fields; do not add unsupported job/report fields or Sheet columns.

## Discovery and evidence

SOSA starts with bounded local Search/Maps, public Instagram/Facebook business pages and suitable local directories, using the tools actually available. Example searches: Fairfax residential cleaning; Burke house cleaning Facebook; Fairfax home cleaning Instagram. Follow business-controlled links and verify identity/location rather than relying only on polished websites at the top of search results. Record the actual query and identity/location checks. Directories are discovery leads, not proof of current operation or first-party contact ownership.

Follow public business links to the website/profile, offering, menu/rates, enquiry/order routes, existing alternatives and current activity. Record what works as well as potential additions. Check exact name/location and bounded website searches; say No verified site found when appropriate. Claim Instagram-only sales or absence of another route only when evidence establishes it.

Use 1–4 approved source URLs per quality job. Capture dates, scope, factual context and exact citations; retain partial coverage and access failures honestly. Inspect the five journey areas: discovery, offer, enquiry/ordering, trust/activity and follow-up. No private content, unrelated personal information, login bypass, forms, business contact or purchases. Workers assess supplied evidence only; the runner retrieves approved business-host pages. Unseen internal tools stay unknown.

## What makes an opportunity

Tie one practical addition or improvement to an observed offering/customer journey. Consider enhancements even when the existing route works. Explain what the addition could enable and how it complements existing alternatives; check for duplication. A small digital presence is a useful discovery signal, not a complete proposal. A generic website/CRM/AI package is insufficient.

Put current facts in observations with citations. In version-2/3 reports, each opportunity supplies observed_basis, a specific proposed_solution, possible_effect as a hypothesis, existing_alternatives, validation_needed and one focused owner_question. Clearly describe whether the proposal is an enhancement or addresses an observed problem. Follow the supplied version's schema exactly; older schemas express checks in their existing owner_question/unknowns fields, without adding fields.

Evaluate a tailored enhancement before choosing No strong public case. If none is defensible after adequate inspection, explain why in unknowns. Unknown goals, budget or buying interest alone are not reasons to dismiss a relevant enhancement. Directory-only/unsupported evidence still cannot qualify an opportunity under the evidence contract.

## Decisions and priority

Research-qualified: a specific supported enhancement/problem opportunity, reviewed current primary operating evidence and a captured first-party public contact route, with every observation/journey finding reviewed. This means worth a discovery conversation, not confirmed purchase or a struggling business. Preserve existing freshness, citation, duplicate and qualification checks. Email is primary; a public business social contact can support research while email discovery continues.

Hold: key identity, activity, contact or required supporting evidence is uncertain. Record the exact next verification step. Unknown budget, interest or private workflows alone do not force Hold when the relevant public context and enhancement are supported.

Pass: a verified campaign mismatch/closed business, or adequate research yields no specific defensible enhancement beyond existing alternatives. Record why. Pass applies to this campaign and can be revisited if information changes; insufficient access/evidence belongs on Hold.

Within the cleaner campaign, first seek the basic/social-first setups described above, then prioritize the clearest tailored idea, credible current activity and a usable contact route. An established cleaner with a stronger specific opening can outrank a basic setup with a weak idea. Owner-expressed goals or a relevant new offering can strengthen the case when actually observed; neither is required. Do not invent revenue, affordability, urgency or a numerical score. Evaluate campaign fit through usable conversations, owner-confirmed needs and suitable projects relative to research effort; the number of reports alone does not establish success.
