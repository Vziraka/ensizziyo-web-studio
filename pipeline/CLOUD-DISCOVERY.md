# Shared public discovery

`shared-operations-desk-v1` extends the canonical mailbox workspace with separately bound public research. Existing profiles do not acquire discovery authority. Research never sends mail, approves a message, dials a phone, enrolls a historical contact or changes an existing business record. Existing inbox, reply proposal and exact delivery controls remain separate.

## Scope and daily accounting

The immutable `cloud_discovery` policy binds site/deploy/origin, expiry, recovery generation, distinct researcher/reviewer identities, reviewed DMV areas and the approved home-service trades. The daily email-qualified target is 100, all seven days, counted in America/New_York time. Twenty-five is a **chunk size**, not the daily ceiling. Candidate, task, attempt and total daily import limits remain finite; a target is not a capacity guarantee.

The trusted coordinator can `queue-daily` with only the current revision. The core derives the chunk ID, remaining target, area, trade and alternating responsible operator from policy and retained state. Pending chunks for that calendar day count against the target. Earlier jobs and charges remain retained; a new date does not reset an uncertain claim. Additional signed call-first requests use independent chunks of at most 25 candidates. Daily counters include historical canonical import events, not merely the new worker's tasks.

An accepted quick-verification card remains **Verification call only**, with public identity/phone evidence and a supported opening. It does not count toward email-qualified supply. Email qualification instead requires the existing detailed report schema, source validation, `validateEvidenceReview`, `validateDecision`, current-operation evidence, first-party public email and supported opportunity. The desk shows email-qualified supply/shortfall, call-first cards and requested candidate-slot shortfall, duplicates and held results separately. Missing evidence produces a hold rather than invented qualification.

## Authority and failures

State starts paused, with no allowance. Signed operators may create bounded tasks, record actual included-usage evidence, pause/resume and import exact completed reviews. Only the trusted server coordinator can queue daily work or publish automatically; a worker credential cannot import. Automatic publication records actor `discovery`, without attributing a human approval that did not occur. Imported responsibility follows the saved task assignment.

Research and review claims require different immutable worker identities and different context IDs. Each claim permanently charges one attempt before external work; the initial research claim also charges its maximum candidate slots. A claim cannot be reassigned, retried or refunded. Source submission and review must bind the exact original task, claim and context. Duplicate HTTP recovery may read an identical saved receipt; it must not create another claim or mutate a prior submission. Unfinished/expired claims stay visible and charged. Pausing increments the epoch, fencing unfinished tasks and their results; resuming does not revive them.

Every active step requires unexpired policy, current pause/epoch, and a finite allowance with a remaining-usage observation **above 30%**. The observation/allowance lasts at most 15 minutes. Below-reserve evidence can be recorded for visibility but cannot resume or claim work. These observations are not a live subscription meter. The earlier below-reserve native Work observation was superseded by a verified owner-triggered reset. Each subsequent activation still requires fresh actual evidence within the fifteen-minute limit; a reset does not establish continuing capacity. No purchase, overage or credential change is authorized by this source increment. Continuous unattended operation needs a verified fresh usage-evidence path; a fifteen-minute manual observation does not establish it.

Conditional storage independently replays each typed transition and checks eligibility again using its own clock immediately before CAS. Imports validate exact artifact bytes again, then deduplicate against all saved records, including stopped businesses, using normalized names, website/contact-source domains, phone numbers, emails and company IDs. Existing records are returned as duplicates and are not rewritten. The whole chunk imports atomically; changed evidence, stale sources, limits or capacity failures leave canonical records unchanged.

## Original evidence and recovery

Submission and review are immutable JSON artifacts, each at most 2,000,000 UTF-8 bytes. Their exact reference is `{sha256, bytes, reference: "cloud-discovery/" + sha256}`, where SHA-256 covers `JSON.stringify(payload)`. Original source captures and detailed reports live in these content-addressed dependencies, not in the task ledger. Services load the original payloads for review/import replay; a client-supplied hash alone establishes nothing.

Call cards retain the existing validator-compatible verification packet, bounded to 24 KB, because `verificationFresh` requires its evidence. The existing import event also retains that packet, so canonical cost can approach twice that bound per call card. A 19 MB whole-state guard rejects partial publication before the canonical envelope limit. Email-qualified cards retain compact reviewed call preparation and exact evidence references. Backups must retain both task and imported-record artifact references. Recovery pauses discovery and advances its epoch while preserving all claims, charges, results and original artifacts.

## Interfaces and verification

The pure core exports `validateCloudDiscoveryPolicy`, `validateCloudDiscoveryState`, `applyCloudDiscoveryTransition`, `cloudDiscoveryStatus`, `assertCloudDiscoveryWorkerContext` and `assertCloudDiscoveryTransitionLive`. Worker context reads validate current authority but grant no mutation authority. Public source text is untrusted evidence, never tool instructions.

Signed desk routes are `/api/cloud-discovery/{create|pause|allowance|import}`. Import callers provide task ID/current revision; the service loads the stored exact artifacts. Worker claim/context/submission/review use their separately authenticated gateway, never browser credentials or UI controls. The Setup panel exposes status, finite usage evidence, pause, call-first requests and assigned completed-review import. Queued work does not prove an enabled schedule or connected worker.

Focused core tests cover closed policies, old-profile denial, 17% reserve hold, distinct identities/contexts, charged claims, pause/deadline/source races, stronger email qualification, history-preserving deduplication, multiple chunks beyond 25/day, Eastern midnight/weekends, coordinator attribution, whole-chunk rollback and storage live-clock fencing. Integration acceptance additionally requires actual canonical conditional storage, signed caller/worker/reviewer/import boundaries, lost-response readback and protected old-profile denials. Browser verification, independent engineering/requirements review, required CI, native scheduled capture plus separate review, actual canonical import/readback and sustainable included capacity remain separate evidence gates. The earlier native PDF proposal proof is not discovery acceptance.

A trusted coordinator may mark an exact reviewed task Held after deterministic import validation fails, bound to its current task hash and revision. The hold retains its reason, actor, timestamp, original artifacts and permanent charges. Held tasks cannot import or cover pending daily supply; later bounded tasks may proceed. Revision conflicts, expired allowance, global canonical capacity, daily import ceilings, an already-filled email target, and transient artifact/storage/CAS failures must not be converted into this terminal hold. Only immutable packet validation and conversion failures receive the invalid-packet classification. Workers and signed operator routes cannot invoke this transition.


## Worker context contract

The context includes concrete `call_packet_schema`, `call_review_schema`, `query_schema` and `source_snapshot.schema` references, alongside the original closed submission/review envelope examples. `business_type` is an object with `category: "home-services"` and one exact task subtype. Capture enums come from the same values used by the source validator: kind `business|social|directory`, method `http|reviewed_page|search_snippet`, coverage `full|partial`. Schema references describe structure; the existing validators remain authoritative for actual source ownership, evidence, quote budgets, freshness, current claims and opening quality.

A source's `checked_at` is **date only**, `YYYY-MM-DD`; its observed `fetched_at` is an ISO timestamp on that date. A query's `checked_at` is a full canonical UTC timestamp with milliseconds during its research claim. A call packet permits at most two sources by default; only `profile_version: 1` permits four, all business captures on the verified first-party host. Detailed research permits one to four distinct sources. HTTP/copied captures require at least 50 text characters; short reviewed anchors retain the existing reviewed-context/partial-coverage requirements. Opening review carries the existing three-alternative maximum, field bounds and `selection_reason` maximum of 1,200 characters.

New public searches whose engine is unspecified use `Public web search`. The candidate factory and new cloud email imports no longer invent `Google Search`. Existing named-provider/Referral labels remain valid, and retained packets, hashes, claims and imported history are not rewritten. Artifact/source hashing, policy/deployment binding, reserve charges, role/context separation, deadlines and whole-state replay are unchanged.

`cloud-discovery-context.test.mjs` constructs complete accepted/rejected call packets from emitted context and submits/reviews/imports them through the real conditional store. It also checks detailed research context, exact artifact readback, historical named provenance, four-source profiles, and rejection of invalid business shapes, enums, dates, source counts, short captures and oversized review reasons without publishing results or refunding claims. This repairs the demonstrated caller contract; it does not rerun or retroactively approve the earlier native proof, deploy a gateway, or establish autonomous research quality/capacity. Final independent requirements and engineering review and CI remain release gates.

## Disabled deployment and rollback

Build the standard desk assets with `node scripts/build-desk-assets.mjs`. The generated discovery clock has no schedule unless the build has `CONTEXT=production` and `EVZ_DISCOVERY_CLOCK_BUILD=shared-operations-desk-v1`. Runtime additionally requires `EVZ_DISCOVERY_CLOCK_RUNTIME=shared-operations-desk-v1`, an exact published deployment and matching canonical binding. It coordinates at most one queue/import/hold transition per five-minute invocation; it neither calls models nor renews usage evidence. Operator pause immediately fences active work. Disable the runtime and publish the default-disabled clock to remove its schedule; retain the canonical namespace and evidence for recovery.

The separate API-only gateway uses `EVZ_DISCOVERY_GATEWAY=bounded-public-research-v1`, `EVZ_DISCOVERY_SOURCE_BINDING`, server-only `EVZ_DISCOVERY_SOURCE_BLOBS_TOKEN`, and `EVZ_DISCOVERY_GATEWAY_POLICY`. The latter has exactly site_id, deploy_id, origin, policy_sha256, research_context, review_context, research_credential_sha256 and review_credential_sha256. Distinct credential hashes and context identities bind each role to the exact source policy. Store only placeholders in repository examples; never expose the source storage token to workers. This source does not provision or broaden that storage credential. The MCP route is `/desk-discovery/mcp`; research/review credentials have no import, approval or delivery tool.
