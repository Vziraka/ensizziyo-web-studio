# Shared mailbox cloud proposals

The approved operations release needs on-demand AI replies without keeping either operator's computer awake. `shared-mailbox-work-v1` extends the shared mailbox review authority with prepared reply tasks, not provider execution. It retains the existing conditional `workspace` object, historical tasks, incoming evidence, manual calling and exact human approvals. Old capabilities cannot gain worker access through an environment switch.

## One authority, separate callers

The immutable manual manifest binds `config.mailbox_work`: exact worker and gateway deployment, credential digest, recovery generation and hard capacity/lease limits. Schema-1 policies retain their absolute expiry and proof retention ceiling. New task entries use `schema_version: 2` in `state.email_draft_tasks`; legacy entries remain unchanged. Drafts require a currently assigned verified reply, completed mailbox coverage and no relevant unresolved/newer incoming source. Ownership, source, style, draft baseline and offered PDFs form the context fingerprint.

Signed desk commands create, cancel and adopt tasks. A separate API-only gateway authenticates a workspace worker and exposes only claim, context, bounded original segments, proposal, failure and receipt operations. It cannot create arbitrary tasks, adopt, approve, send, read Gmail credentials or choose arbitrary URLs/storage keys. Public discovery and recurring execution are not implemented by this reply-only increment.

A stable worker credential identifies the connection; each claim receives a separate short-lived lease. Claim request IDs reconcile lost acknowledgements, lease fencing increases monotonically, and expired attempts cannot silently reclaim work. Credential/policy/recovery changes revoke old authority. Retained receipts remain readable after task or policy expiry under the unchanged worker identity; expiry never permits another mutation. Immediately before conditional writes, the store rechecks live policy and lease expiry against its trusted clock. Conditional replay independently reproduces each transition and rejects unrelated state changes. Failed or uncertain attempts continue to consume their claim allowance.

An assigned operator adopts an exact proposal into an **unapproved** reply draft. The source, catalogue and actual selected PDF bytes are checked before adoption; fresh conditional replay checks them again where applicable. Existing exact-content approval remains separate. A relevant newer or unresolved mailbox source also invalidates the adopted draft and prevents its approval. Signed operators can inspect only the exact saved attachment bytes through the existing PDF preview/download route. Edits, ownership changes and changed source/baselines invalidate the relevant context. There is no outgoing transport in this capability.

The worker returns body text without the desk-managed signature. During mailbox-task adoption, one exact trailing copy of that signature is removed before composing the unapproved draft; a signature-only body is rejected. The raw proposal, its hash and adoption-event input remain unchanged. Quoted, middle and near-match text is not stripped. No historical approval or sending-time content is rewritten.

## Renewable manual allowances

Only `shared-operations-desk-v1` may explicitly opt into worker policy schema 2. It keeps the worker/gateway/credential/recovery identities, `max_claims_per_day` and `lease_minutes`, and replaces `expires_at`/`max_tasks` with `max_claims_per_allowance`, `max_allowance_minutes`, `max_open_tasks` and `max_retained_tasks`. Limits remain finite; retained tasks are bounded by the existing 100-record ceiling and the whole-workspace 20 MB limit. A full history is a maintenance hold, never permission to delete evidence. Schema 1 and other capabilities retain their denials.

Signed, CSRF/revision-checked `/api/mailbox-work/allowance` accepts `{revision,allowance:{id,evidence_ref,observed_at,expires_at,included_only:true,max_claims}}`. The observation must be no older than fifteen minutes and its budget/duration must fit the immutable policy. `/pause` accepts `{revision,paused,reason}`. Either signed operator may stop claims; a worker cannot grant or resume itself. New controls start paused. Saving an allowance does not unpause or invoke a model. Included usage and the applicable reserve are manually reviewed, not live metered. The ordinary reserve stays 30%; record an explicitly authorized task exception in the evidence reference rather than changing that default.

`state.mailbox_work` retains every allowance and permanent `used_claims` charge. Each new lease names its original `allowance_id` and authority `epoch`, and expires at the earlier of its allowance and policy lease duration. Adoption/cancellation frees an open slot, not a history slot or charge. Daily counts and unique request/lease/fence identities span all retained grants and policies. Ordinary renewal refuses a live Claimed task. Pause revokes its epoch; unpause cannot restore that lease. An expired/revoked claim requires explicit cancellation before replacement. The six MCP tools are unchanged. Receipt reads outlive expiry, and an accepted current-policy Review proposal remains adoptable after grant expiry when its sources are still current.

Normal renewal never changes config, binding or gateway. A one-time reviewed upgrade imports exact nonempty G1 state unchanged. Historical tasks stay readable and cannot claim under the new policy. The assigned operator explicitly cancels a historical claim or prepares a replacement from checked current sources; its old result/lease remain retained. A retired schema-2 control ledger needs signed `/rebind` with `{revision,previous_policy_sha256,authorized:true}` while paused. Rebind records the old policy hash, advances epoch, clears the active allowance pointer and stays paused. A new allowance is a separate decision. Complete restore also clears the active pointer, advances epoch and pauses without resetting charges or lease evidence.

The store independently replays typed controls and repeats trusted-clock/source/allowance checks after awaits and at the actual SDK transport boundary. A delayed signed-write lookup cannot admit an expired claim/result. Setup displays allowance/day/open/history capacity and expiry. Preparing a task is separate from authorizing a model claim; a bounded task can wait for a reviewed allowance.

## Original bytes

The existing immutable original store keeps its 1,000,000-byte storage chunks. The task transport returns at most 262,144 bytes per segment, with offset, segment digest, total length and whole-file digest. A segment may end at a storage-chunk boundary. Workers follow the returned next offset, reconstruct the entire file, verify its whole-file digest and inspect the actual PDF before proposing it. Metadata or extracted text alone does not prove visual inspection. Existing limits remain two selected PDFs and 20,000,000 combined attachment bytes; oversized originals are not silently compressed or represented as complete.

## Gateway configuration and hosting

The desk remains private. The proposed API gateway uses a separate Netlify project, without desk assets or anonymous business-data responses. Netlify's project-protection gate precedes application authentication, so placing this endpoint behind the existing private desk gate would not make it callable by Work. Cross-project Blobs access uses an explicitly authorized server-side storage token; it must never be sent to the cloud worker. Its actual scope must be reviewed before deployment—do not describe an account token as store-scoped.

The gateway entry point is `/desk-work/mcp`, disabled unless `EVZ_MAILBOX_WORK_GATEWAY=scoped-reply-worker-v1`. Private environment configuration also supplies `EVZ_MAILBOX_WORK_SOURCE_BINDING`, `EVZ_MAILBOX_WORK_SOURCE_BLOBS_TOKEN`, `EVZ_MAILBOX_WORK_CREDENTIAL_SHA256` and a separate random `EVZ_MAILBOX_WORK_LEASE_SECRET`. Invalid worker credentials reject before source storage access. Actual trusted gateway site/deploy/origin must match the source policy; never spoof the desk's metadata. Deploying source does not create a connection or run a task.

Included Work usage was observed on the actual account, but no authoritative automatic allowance reader has been established. Finite claims do not prove a percentage reserve, especially while other chats use the same subscription. No purchase, overage, paid API or paid-provider fallback is authorized by this implementation. Sustainable 100-business/day research and unattended usage-reserve enforcement remain separate release gates.

## Verification and rollback

From `pipeline`, run `node --test mailbox-work.test.mjs mailbox-work-service.test.mjs mailbox-renewal.test.mjs mailbox-renewal-service.test.mjs shared-manual-records.test.mjs`, `npm run check`, then required full CI. Pure tests cover retained-state corruption, source/ownership/classification changes, lease expiry, revocation, fencing, claim limits and replay. Integration tests use the actual MCP/Blobs SDKs, conditional store and signed desk handler to reconstruct a multi-chunk PDF, reconcile a lost claim acknowledgement, save/adopt proposals and complete two separately approved replies across renewed allowances. They also cover nonempty legacy rebind, original same-account reconciliation and restore revocation. External services are substituted at their boundaries.

Browser recipe: use a synthetic production-shaped mailbox with completed intake, an assigned reply and approved original. In Call & reply reviews, request a proposal; have the synthetic worker claim/read/submit; refresh the task; inspect sources, uncertainty and PDFs; adopt the proposal and verify Draft; approve its exact revision and reload to verify Approved; sending held. Check other-operator denial and narrow layout. Stop the fixture server and retain evidence outside Git.

For renewable controls use the account-free actual-handler fixture in `fixtures/manual-mailbox-renewal.mjs`. The coordinator's local server supplies fictional owner/Friend keys and memory provider/storage adapters; it has no external fetch or real account connection. In Setup, explicitly rebind paused historical controls if shown, record a finite allowance, verify saving does not resume, then resume separately. Complete two proposal/adoption/exact-send journeys with distinct sources and fresh grants; verify both permanent charges/receipts, reload, either-role pause and 400 CSS pixels. Close only the coordinator's loopback fixture process. Local HTTP cookie adaptation is not production TLS/access or actual native/cloud proof; private evidence owns that server command and screenshots.

Before claiming native cloud acceptance, verify the authenticated deployed endpoint, install the exact connection, execute one finite fictional Work task, inspect its actual PDF reconstruction, read the result independently in the desk and test interrupted/replayed claims. Actual computer-off operation and independent teammate identity remain separate checks. Rollback disables the gateway flag and revokes/replaces its worker policy; preserve task and result history. Existing private desk access must remain unchanged.
