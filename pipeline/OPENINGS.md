# Source-backed opening selection

New discovery retains the existing single bounded source-review call. That review returns up to three alternatives, one chosen ID and a reason comparing their evidence and relevance. Each alternative names one service, a specific customer difficulty or meaningful customer step, its exact retained primary quote/hash, a possible improvement, a short opening and one owner question. A generic offering/locality question or four-service menu is insufficient for a new pitch card. Missing or weak openings stay **Held** with the missing check; the batch does not expand to fill the daily target.

Literal validation binds every alternative to the retained business/social capture. The review must judge whether the quote actually supports the difficulty and proposed question; matching text alone cannot establish that meaning. Possible improvements and benefits remain hypotheses, and owner needs, current internal tools, budget and interest remain unconfirmed. Website openings require a supported customer difficulty. Dated appearance alone fails. Current discovery captures page text, so mobile/layout/usability claims require separate visual inspection and stay held here; no screenshot is silently fabricated.

The [current playbook](RESEARCH.md#focused-opening-acceptance) separates observed process, tentative improvement and one conversational process question. The operational contract rejects solution commands, questions such as “How can we automate …?”, generic questions without a specific source term, and unsupported visual/functional website claims. These are conservative contract checks; an accepted shape, quote/hash or separate same-model review cannot establish semantic quality. The real fictional Gateway author/reviewer failures remain negative evidence, with their original receipts and exhausted approval unchanged. New live quality remains unverified.

The chosen opening appears on the call card. Open **Research**, then expand **Research, sources and what is still unknown** to see the difficulty, exact quote, source, possible improvement and selection reason. Either signed operator may choose another supported alternative and record why. That event binds the exact assessment hash and candidate ID; stale, invented or stopped-contact selections fail. The retained verification packet and qualification remain unchanged. The call card and project-context handoff use the operator's current selection. Selecting an opening never places a call, approves an email or authorizes sending.

Each new saved call retains the exact selected opening and selecting operator. An unanswered follow-up uses that opening's source quote and process question, with its matching service example. Later selection changes preserve the saved message. Connected follow-up can follow the reviewed conversation instead. Private notes are excluded from automatic copy; a single unanswered call says “I tried calling” and establishes neither an incoming call nor chronic lost business. An assessment failing current checks displays a hold instead of falling back to its rejected opening. Historical call/message records are not rewritten.

Existing verification records retain their original evidence and explicit quick-verification label; they are not silently regraded as strong pitches. Detailed research receives the current playbook and instructions to choose the strongest supported opportunity, require a website customer difficulty and hold unsupported visual claims. Previously accepted detailed reports remain history. Actual provider quality on representative businesses, strongest-opening consistency across older records and measured yield/usage remain unverified; these source changes are not proof of live research performance.

Verify with `node --test opening-policy.test.mjs discovery-batches.test.mjs follow-up.test.mjs gateway-proof.test.mjs` from `pipeline`, then syntax/full lifecycle checks. Fixtures include the actual fictional Gateway negatives, wrong sources/hashes/quotes, generic and presumptive questions, supported website-content/phone-process cases, operator override through real HTTP, call snapshots, restart, immutable prior email, stop/stale rejection and bounded discovery without another review call. Browser rehearsal checks selection, quote/reason, override, unanswered preview/save consistency, held invalid assessments and narrow layout using fictional state. No real provider inference or prospect contact is required.
